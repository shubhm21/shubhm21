### 🚀 Featured projects

#### 📦 [Supply Chain & Inventory Optimization](https://github.com/shubhm21/supply-chain-analytics)
`Python` `SQL` `scikit-learn` `Power BI` `Excel`
End-to-end inventory analytics for a 150-SKU distributor (133K sales, 110K inventory snapshots).
- Quantified **$2.34M of revenue lost to stockouts** and traced it to stale reorder points and two unreliable suppliers
- Back-tested 4 forecasting models — ensemble hit **14% WAPE** on top products
- Designed a safety-stock / ROP / EOQ policy that back-tests to **−88% stockouts with 4.8% less inventory**
- Delivered an Excel planning tool with live formulas and a 4-page Power BI dashboard

#### 💳 [Card Portfolio Risk Analysis](https://github.com/shubhm21/card-portfolio-analysis)
`Python` `SQL` `Power BI` `Data Quality`
Security and lifecycle analysis of 6,146 cards held by 2,000 clients.
- Built a star-schema SQLite warehouse and 11 business queries (CTEs, window functions)
- Identified a **2,601-card reissue pipeline (~$37M of limit)** and 94 clients for priority outreach
- Uncovered a data-migration artifact that was **understating security risk by ~7 points**
- Masked card numbers / dropped CVVs at ingestion — privacy by design
